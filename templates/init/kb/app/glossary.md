# Glossary

Business words that appear in JIRA cards but not on screen.

The planner reads a card that says "the user's GAQ status is unfit" and has no
idea what GAQ is, which value counts as unfit, or where it shows in the UI.
One line here is the difference between a plan grounded in your domain and one
built on a guess.

Add a term when you find yourself explaining it to a new joiner. Delete the
example above.
